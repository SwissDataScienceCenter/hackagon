# Teams

:::{admonition} Participant's Teams

Who works on which project. Appears once teams exist and the owners publish
them. It shows:

- [All teams](#all-teams): grouped by project
- [A team](#a-team): its members and its project

:::

![The Teams page of a hackathon, as a participant sees it](../static/screenshots/member-teams.png)

## All teams

- Teams grouped under their project.
- Each team shows its project's image. Without one it shows the project's
  initials.
- The project under the team name links to its [project](member-projects.md).
- Each team shows its members. Click a member to open their profile.
- Click {guilabel}`View` to open [a team](#a-team).

## A team

- Its members. Click a member to open their profile.
- Its project, as on the [projects](member-projects.md) page. Click
  {guilabel}`View` to open it.
- Whether the team has handed in its work. Click {guilabel}`View entry` to read
  it on [Submissions](member-submissions.md).
- Hand in your own team's work on [Our submission](member-our-submission.md).
- Read what each team handed in on [Submissions](member-submissions.md).

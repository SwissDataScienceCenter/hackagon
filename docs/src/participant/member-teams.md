# Teams

:::{admonition} Participant's Teams

Who works on which project. Appears once teams exist and the owners publish
them. It shows:

- [All teams](#all-teams): grouped by project
- [A team](#a-team): its members and its entry

:::

![The Teams page of a hackathon, as a participant sees it](../static/screenshots/member-teams.png)

## All teams

- Teams grouped under their project; the project title links to its
  [project](member-projects.md).
- Each team shows its members and its final entry, if it has one.

## A team

- Members, project and **Read the full proposal**.
- The team's final entry, or a note that it has none yet.
- Hand in your own team's work on [Submissions](member-submissions.md).

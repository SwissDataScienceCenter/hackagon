# Participants

This guide is for the people taking part in a hackathon: registering, finding a
project and a team, handing in work, and voting.

What you see in a hackathon's menu depends on what its owners have switched on,
so some of these pages appear only at certain stages.

## Registering

- Click {guilabel}`Register` on the hackathon's public page.
- On a public hackathon you wait until an owner approves you.
- While you wait, click {guilabel}`Cancel registration` on the public page to
  come off the list. Your answers are deleted.
- Once you are in, you leave from your own profile. See
  [Leave the hackathon](member-participants.md#leave-the-hackathon).

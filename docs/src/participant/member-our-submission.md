# Our submission

:::{admonition} Participant's Our submission

Where your team hands in its work. Appears in the menu once you are on a
[team](member-teams.md). It shows:

- [Hand in work](#hand-in-work): one submission per team, changed in place

It might also show:

- [Submitting closed](#submitting-closed): your team's submission, read only

:::

## Hand in work

- You can hand in work only while submitting is open. The
  [Overview](member-overview.md) shows whether it is.
- Enter a repository link, an attachments folder link or a description. Then
  click {guilabel}`Save`.
- Saving replaces your team's submission. Anyone on the team can change it.
  Everyone sees the latest save.
- The bar above the fields says when it was last saved, and by whom.
- As soon as you change something, the bar lists what is not saved yet. The
  changed fields are marked.
- Click {guilabel}`Discard` to go back to what is saved.
- If you leave the page with unsaved changes, it asks first.
- What you save is what others read on [Submissions](member-submissions.md) and
  [vote](member-voting.md) on.

## Submitting closed

- Once the owners close submitting, the page shows your team's submission
  without the form. It can no longer be changed.

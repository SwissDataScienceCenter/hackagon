# About

:::{admonition} Participant's About

What the hackathon is. First in the menu. It shows:

- [Your hackathon](#your-hackathon): its name, dates and number of participants
- [The description](#the-description): in the owners' own words

It might also show:

- [The phases](#the-phases): if the hackathon has phases

:::

## Your hackathon

- The hackathon's name and picture, if the owners added one.
- Start and end date, and how many participants it has.
- Whether you are registered or on the waitlist.

## The phases

- Every phase of the hackathon in order, with the running one marked.
- See [Timeline](member-timeline.md) for their dates.

## The description

- The description the owners wrote for the hackathon.
- Click {guilabel}`Overview` in the menu to see what is open right now.

# Submissions

:::{admonition} Participant's Submissions

Where your team hands in its work. It shows, for each of your teams:

- [Hand in work](#hand-in-work): save a draft
- [Finalize](#finalize): make a version your team's entry
- [Earlier versions](#earlier-versions): everything handed in so far

:::

![The Submissions page of a hackathon, as a participant sees it](../static/screenshots/member-submissions.png)

## Hand in work

- Only while submitting is open for the hackathon, see [overview](member-overview.md), and only once you are on a
  [team](member-teams.md).
- Enter a link or notes, then click on **Save draft**. Every draft is a new version.

## Finalize

- **Finalize this version** makes a draft your team's entry.
- Anyone on the team can do it, and it cannot be undone.
- The final entry is what others [vote](member-voting.md) on.
- There can be several final entries. The last one will be the one that counts for the team during voting.

## Earlier versions

- All versions your team handed in are folded away.

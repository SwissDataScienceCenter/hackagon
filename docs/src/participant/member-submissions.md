# Submissions

:::{admonition} Participant's Submissions

What every team has handed in, once the owners publish the teams. It shows:

- [Your team](#your-team): your team's submission, first
- [Other teams](#other-teams): every other team that has handed something in
- [Submission page](#submission-page): one team's submission in full

:::

![The Submissions page of a hackathon, as a participant sees it](../static/screenshots/member-submissions.png)

## Your team

- Your team's submission is at the top, as everyone else sees it.
- Until your team saves something, it says {guilabel}`Not submitted yet`.
- Click {guilabel}`Edit` to change it on
  [Our submission](member-our-submission.md). The button shows only while
  submitting is open.

## Other teams

- Every other team that has handed something in, grouped by project.
- The heading counts how many teams have submitted.
- Each submission shows the start of its description, its team, and when it was
  last updated.
- A submission without a description shows its link instead.
- Click {guilabel}`View` to read it in full on its
  [submission page](#submission-page).

## Submission page

- The project's name, the team, and when the submission was last updated.
- The full description and the submission's links.
- The team, as on the [teams](member-teams.md) page, and the project, as on the
  [projects](member-projects.md) page. Click {guilabel}`View` to open either.
- On your own team's page, click {guilabel}`Edit` to change it on
  [Our submission](member-our-submission.md). The button shows only while
  submitting is open.
- The back link returns to the page you came from. Opened on its own, it goes to
  Submissions.

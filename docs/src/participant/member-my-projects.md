# My projects

:::{admonition} Participant's My projects

The projects you proposed in this hackathon. Appears in the menu while the
owners take proposals, or once you have proposed one. It shows:

- [Your projects](#your-projects): at every status, newest first

It might also show:

- [Propose a project](#propose-a-project): while the owners take proposals

:::

## Your projects

- A project still waiting for review is marked {guilabel}`Proposed`. One the
  owners turned down is marked {guilabel}`Rejected`. An approved one has no
  mark.
- Only approved projects also appear on [Projects](member-projects.md).
- The entry stays in the menu after the owners stop taking proposals.
- Click {guilabel}`View` to open a project. For a rejected one, it shows the
  owner's reason.
- Click {guilabel}`Edit` to change a project at any status. Editing does not
  change its status.
- An owner of the hackathon sees this entry only if they also take part in it.

## Propose a project

- Before your first proposal, click {guilabel}`Propose your first project`.
  After that, click {guilabel}`Propose a Project` at the top.
- Enter a title, a description, and a track if the hackathon has tracks.
- You come back here, where the new proposal is marked {guilabel}`Proposed`.
- An owner reviews it before it appears on Projects, see
  [Manage Projects](../owner/manage-projects.md).

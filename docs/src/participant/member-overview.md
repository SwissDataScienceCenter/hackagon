# Overview

:::{admonition} Participant's Overview

The first page of a hackathon. It shows:

- [Your next steps](#your-next-steps): what is open now, and what you have done
- [Your team](#your-team): your team, or how to find one

It might also show:

- [The current phase](#the-current-phase): if the hackathon has phases
- [Projects](#projects): once projects are approved

The hackathon's dates, picture and description are on its own page, see
[About](member-about.md).

:::

![The Overview page of a hackathon, as a participant sees it](../static/screenshots/member-overview.png)

## Your next steps

- One row per activity the owners have opened:
  [propose projects](member-my-projects.md#propose-a-project),
  [choose preferred projects](member-projects.md#preferences),
  [see teams](member-teams.md), [submit work](member-submissions.md),
  [vote](member-voting.md), [see results](member-results.md).
- An empty circle marks something still to do. Click the row to do it.
- A check marks something you have done, for example "You proposed 2 projects".
  Click the row to see it or change it.
- Submitting needs a team. Without one the row says so and has no link.
- "Nothing is open right now" when the owners have opened nothing.
- On the waitlist the links stay closed until an owner approves you.

## The current phase

- A box of its own, above your next steps, while a phase is running.
- Name, dates, countdown and description of the phase.
- Phases are for orientation only, see [Timeline](member-timeline.md).

## Your team

- Your team's members, its project and whether it has handed in work.
- Click {guilabel}`Submit your work` to go to
  [Submissions](member-submissions.md) while submitting is open.
- Not on a team yet: click {guilabel}`Browse the projects` to go to
  [Projects](member-projects.md).

## Projects

- The number of approved projects, per track if there are tracks.
- Click {guilabel}`View all` to go to [Projects](member-projects.md).

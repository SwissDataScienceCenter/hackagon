# Results

:::{admonition} Participant's Results

The outcome of the voting. Appears once the owners publish the results. It
shows:

- [Placings](#placings): per vote category
- [All entries](#all-entries): every team that entered

:::

![The Results page of a hackathon, as a participant sees it](../static/screenshots/member-results.png)

## Placings

- Per category: the placed entries with medal and score.
- Click {guilabel}`View entry` to open the [team](member-teams.md) and its
  entry.

## All entries

- Every team that entered. Yours is marked {guilabel}`Your team`.

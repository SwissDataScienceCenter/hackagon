# Manage Teams

:::{admonition} Owner's Manage Teams

Where you form the teams. It shows:

- [Projects](#projects): each approved project with its teams
- [Unassigned](#unassigned): confirmed participants without a team
- [Filters and cards](#filters-and-cards): registration answers and preferences
- [Spreadsheet](#spreadsheet): download and upload a CSV

It might also show:

- [Teams are fixed](#teams-are-fixed): once teams are published or work is
  handed in

:::

![The Manage Teams page of a hackathon, as its owner sees it](../static/screenshots/manage-teams.png)

## Projects

- One card per [approved project](manage-projects.md), with **N want in** and
  its teams.
- **+ Add Team**; rename or delete a team.
- Drag people between teams.

## Unassigned

- Filter by registration answer or preferred project; **Reset filters**.

## Filters and cards

- **Registration questions**: short names, filters and **Show on cards**, see
  [Manage Forms](manage-forms.md).
- **Project preferences**: what participants chose on
  [Projects](../participant/member-projects.md#preferences).
- **Find a person** searches the page.

## Spreadsheet

- **Download CSV**: everyone with preferences and answers.
- Edit the `project` and `team` columns, then **Upload CSV**; it replaces all
  teams.
- **How assignment works** explains the format.

## Saving

- Nothing is stored until **Save**; **Discard** drops your changes.
- Saving deletes empty teams.
- Participants see the teams only once you let them, see
  [Settings](manage-settings.md).

## Teams are fixed

- Once teams are published or a team has handed in work: no upload, no deleting
  teams. Moving people, renaming and adding teams still work.

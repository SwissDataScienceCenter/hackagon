# Manage Tracks

Adding, changing and removing tracks. It appears once the hackathon has a track.

![The Manage Tracks page of a hackathon, as its owner sees it](../static/screenshots/manage-tracks.png)

:::{admonition} Tracks are optional

Hackathons without tracks are perfectly fine.

:::

Tracks group projects, for example by theme. Once a hackathon has a track,
**Manage Tracks** appears in the menu, where you add tracks and edit their
descriptions.

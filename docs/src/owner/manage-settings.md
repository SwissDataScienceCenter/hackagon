# Settings

:::{admonition} Owner's Settings

The first page under **Manage Hackathon**. It shows:

- [What participants can do](#what-participants-can-do): the checkboxes that
  steer the hackathon
- **Where the hackathon is**: the current and next phase, see
  [Manage Timeline](manage-timeline.md)
- **Tracks**: optional, see [Manage Tracks](manage-tracks.md)
- **Manage**: a tile for every other page under **Manage Hackathon**

:::

![The Settings page of a hackathon, as its owner sees it](../static/screenshots/manage-settings.png)

## What participants can do

:::{important}

These checkboxes steer the hackathon: they decide what participants can see and
do. When a hackathon is created they are all off, so participants can do nothing
yet, not even register. Use and change them with care.

:::

The checkboxes are grouped into steps, in the order a hackathon usually runs.
Some steps are optional, and some only point you to the page where you prepare
the next one. Moving the hackathon to another phase never changes them.

| Step                   | Checkbox                               | When it is on, participants can …                                                                                                       | Good to know                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Register            | Open registration                      | join the hackathon. On a public hackathon they wait until you approve them. On a private one, a valid invitation lets them straight in. | While it is off, invitation links don't work either. Nobody can join once the end date has passed. See [Manage Forms](manage-forms.md), [Invitations](manage-invites.md) and [Manage Participants](manage-participants.md).            |
| 2. Projects            | Participants can propose projects      | propose projects for you to review.                                                                                                     | Optional: leave it off if you add all projects yourself. See [Manage Projects](manage-projects.md) and [Projects](../participant/member-projects.md).                                                                                  |
| 3. Project preferences | Participants can voice preferences     | mark the projects they would like to work on.                                                                                           | Turning it off freezes the preferences as they are. See [Projects](../participant/member-projects.md).                                                                                                                                 |
| 4. Team assignments    | —                                      | —                                                                                                                                       | You form the teams. Participants don't see them until the next step. See [Manage Teams](manage-teams.md).                                                                                                                              |
| 5. Hacking starts      | Participants can see teams             | see the teams and who is on them.                                                                                                       | While it is off, participants can't see even their own team. See [Teams](../participant/member-teams.md).                                                                                                                              |
|                        | Participants can submit for their team | hand in work for their team's project.                                                                                                  | Only people on a team can submit. Turning it off stops new submissions, but a team can still finalize a draft it has started. See [Manage Submissions](manage-submissions.md) and [Submissions](../participant/member-submissions.md). |
| 6. Set up voting       | —                                      | —                                                                                                                                       | Create the vote categories and decide who votes in them. See [Manage Voting](manage-voting.md).                                                                                                                                        |
| 7. Voting starts       | Participants can vote                  | vote, and see every team's submissions instead of only their own team's.                                                                | This opens all vote categories at once, and nobody can vote for their own team. Switch it off to close voting before you make the results final. See [Manage Voting](manage-voting.md) and [Voting](../participant/member-voting.md).  |
| 8. Publish results     | Publish results                        | see the vote results.                                                                                                                   | Make the results final on [Manage Voting](manage-voting.md) first. This checkbox then shows them. You always see the results yourself. See [Results](../participant/member-results.md).                                                |

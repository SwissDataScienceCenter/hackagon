# Manage Pages

:::{admonition} Owner's Manage Pages

The hackathon's own pages, such as rules or a schedule. It shows:

- [Pages](#pages): every page, in menu order
- [A page](#a-page): title, visibility and content

:::

![The Manage Pages page of a hackathon, as its owner sees it](../static/screenshots/manage-pages.png)

## Pages

- Drag or use the arrows to change the order in the menu.
- The eye shows or hides a page for participants.
- **View** shows it as participants see it; **Edit** changes it.

## A page

- **New page**: **Title**, **Visible to participants**, **Content** in Markdown.
- Visible pages appear in every participant's menu.
- **Delete this page**.

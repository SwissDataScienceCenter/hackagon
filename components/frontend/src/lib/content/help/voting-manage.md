A voting round has four steps, in this order.

1. **Set up categories.** A category is one question people vote on, such as
   "Best Demo". Once anyone has voted in it, it is locked and can no longer be
   changed.
2. **Open voting, then close it.** Participants can vote while it is open.
   Switch "Vote" in Settings.
3. **Make the results final.** Once voting is closed, use Make results final
   further down this page. It is done once, for every category.
4. **Publish the results.** This shows participants the final results of every
   category, once they are final. Switch "View results" in Settings.
